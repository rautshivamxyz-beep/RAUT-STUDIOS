About the studio

Raut Studio is a one-person software studio. This site is the front door to everything I publish — apps, builds, and the odd loose file. No store, no account, no middleman.

Most software asks you to hand over an account, a connection and a copy of your data before it does anything useful. The apps here go the other way: the work happens on your phone or your PC, and nothing is sent anywhere to make it happen.

That constraint is the whole design. It is why NOVA can answer a question on a flight, why KIDVERSE keeps working when the Wi-Fi drops, and why none of these apps have a sign-up screen.

What gets published here

  • NOVA — an offline AI assistant for Android, with a matching Windows desktop build.
  • KIDVERSE — a learning playground for children around ages 6–10, with math, science and a bilingual library.
  • NOVA Productivity — a local-first dashboard for tasks, goals and study material.
  • Everything else — checksums, patches, archives and documents. Not every file is an app, and the files page says what each one is.

How releases are published

There is no app store in the middle. Builds are published as files on GitHub Releases, and this site is a clear front door to them: one list, real version numbers, real file sizes, and checksums where they matter.

Because there is no store, updates arrive the same way — you download the newer build. Delta patches are published for people who are only a version or two behind, so they do not re-download the whole thing.

Status, honestly

NOVA is the mature project, at version 9.x with regular builds. KIDVERSE and NOVA Productivity are earlier work: usable, but still moving. Where a build is not published yet, the app card links to the source instead of pretending a download exists.

Get in touch

The fastest route is GitHub. Issues on a repository reach the right project directly, and pull requests are welcome.
